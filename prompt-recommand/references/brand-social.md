# 品牌社群類

Use for marketing, brand communication, social content, campaign visuals, and commercial identity. Select the nearest archetype and ask for its required slots.

| ID | Archetype | Required slots | Recipe anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| B3 | 商品形象照 | product reference, must-preserve features, audience, setting, light | Clean commercial still life, believable contact shadow, material fidelity, brand-appropriate setting. |

## Category guidance

- Ask for brand assets, exact wording, and intended platform before stylistic polish.
- For B2, B4, and B6, optimize for reading at final display size.
- For B7 and B10, specify series-wide constants and per-item variables.
- For B9, avoid copying existing marks; request original concepts and simple reproducible geometry.
- For B11, do not invent KPIs or strategy claims. Use user-provided or verified data.
