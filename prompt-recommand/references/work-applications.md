# 工作應用類

Use for professional communication, presentations, structured explanation, and business information design.

| ID | Archetype | Required slots | Recipe anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 | 商業簡報封面 | proposal topic, company, audience, brand colors, ratio | Consultant/technology presentation feel, concise title zone, modern geometry, generous whitespace. |
| W2 | 公司介紹圖卡 | mission, services, differentiators, proof points | Turn company information into a clean hierarchy with one visual theme and scannable sections. |
| W3 | 產品比較表 | options, criteria, values, highlighted choice | Clear columns, comparable rows, icons used sparingly, honest emphasis on key differences. |
| W4 | Q&A 問答圖卡 | verified questions and answers, audience, channel | Pair questions and concise answers in repeatable cards with strong labels and reading order. |
| W5 | App Icon 設計 | app function, audience, symbol, palette, platform | One recognizable geometric symbol, clean silhouette at small sizes, restrained colors; show concept logic outside the icon. |
| W6 | 操作流程圖解 | process, ordered steps, decision points, icons | Numbered flow with arrows, distinct start/end, short labels, and decision branches when needed. |
| W7 | 數據資訊圖 | source data, key insight, chart types, audience | Lead with the takeaway; use accurate scales, limited chart types, and visible labels. |
| W8 | 工作 Checklist 清單 | occasion, sections, items, checkbox behavior | Practical printable list with grouped tasks, clear checkboxes, and enough writing space. |
| W9 | 部落格文章首圖 | article title, theme, audience, site style, ratio | Editorial hero image with a quiet reading mood, one metaphor, and safe title area. |
| W10 | 社群分享卡片 | exact quote, attribution, theme, brand style | Make the quote dominant, preserve punctuation, use subtle background and restrained supporting graphics. |

## Category guidance

- Validate every fact, label, comparison value, and sequence before visualizing it.
- Prefer fewer words and stronger hierarchy over dense generated text.
- When the result will be edited later, ask for clean modular blocks and generous text-safe areas.
- For W5, test the concept mentally at favicon size; visual detail that disappears at small size is noise.
