# 生活實用類

Use for everyday planning, learning, travel, food, journaling, and saveable personal information graphics.

| ID | Archetype | Required slots | Recipe anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | 菜單設計 | venue, categories, verified items/prices, style, format | Warm legible menu with clear sections, restrained food illustrations, and exact prices. |
| L2 | 季節月曆 | month/year, locale, holidays, theme, destination | Accurate calendar grid first; seasonal illustration, soft palette, and wallpaper/print ratio second. |
| L3 | 旅遊行程規劃表 | destination, dates, nights, interests, pace, transport, lodging | Plan by geographic clusters and crowd patterns, then turn the verified itinerary into a magazine-style guide and poster. |
| L4 | 編織小人氣象圖 | city, date, verified weather, landmarks, foods | Central knitted weather board surrounded by a crochet miniature city, Q-version travelers, landmarks, and local food. |
| L5 | 讀書筆記風 | source content, title, takeaways, sections | Handwritten title, underlines, sticky-note blocks, arrows, small doodles, real study-note rhythm and whitespace. |
| L6 | 時間管理規劃圖 | time span, activities, durations, priorities | Timeline or block schedule with distinguishable color coding and productivity-app clarity. |
| L7 | 美食插圖 | dish, ingredients, plating, venue mood | Appetizing food texture, believable plating, warm restaurant light, menu-ready composition. |
| L8 | 日系白邊手繪風 | source photo, objects, annotation language, tone | White one-line contours, arrows/dashes, short diary-like handwritten notes, limited decorative marks. |
| L9 | 一週習慣追蹤表 | habits, week start, check method, style | Simple seven-day grid, small icons, tactile stationery feel, generous tick areas. |
| L10 | 電影觀後感筆記 | film, rating, characters, quote, review | Filmic review card with rating, character notes, one short lawful quote supplied by the user, and reflective summary. |

## Category guidance

- L2 and L4 require current, locale-correct data; verify dates and weather before prompting.
- L3 is a multi-stage task: research/plan → narrative rewrite → visual prompt. Keep the stages explicit and do not fabricate opening hours or transit facts.
- L8 requires inspecting the attached photo before generating object annotations.
- Use exact Traditional Chinese for menus, schedules, and trackers; keep generated on-image text concise.
